Partner agency logos
====================
Drop the official logo files in this folder using these exact names
(PNG with transparent background works best, roughly square):

  doh.png    - Department of Health
  ocd.png    - Office of Civil Defense
  eamc.png   - East Avenue Medical Center
  dilg.png   - Department of the Interior and Local Government

Until a file is added, the site shows the agency's acronym in its place.
